Product-Market Fit

Growth only works as well as the product beneath it. We tell you, objectively, how strong that foundation is.

Every growth effort is ultimately a consequence of product-market fit. Strong fit makes every rupee work harder. Weak fit turns growth spend into a leaking bucket — and no amount of optimisation downstream will fix it.

Three places value leaks

Companies lose user value at acquisition, at retention and at wallet share. Because growth drives all three, it should co-own the diagnosis — not just what the numbers say, but why people aren’t adopting, staying or using more.

Why an outside eye matters

Teams close to a product are the people least able to judge it. Symptoms get explained away; narratives fill the gap where evidence should be. We have looked at many new products, verticals and companies at launch, and have often been the lone voice calling out missing fit. Those calls have held up.

Experiment at the level that matters

Most testing happens at the surface — a colour, a layout, a creative variant. The questions that decide fit sit higher up:

  • Consumer access — can you reach the right users, through which routes, at what cost?
  • Value proposition — which promise actually changes behaviour?
  • Reasons to believe — what makes a user trust that promise enough to act?
  • The user and the moment — for whom, and when, does the product genuinely win?

We test themes, not colours. A better button never rescued a weak promise.

What you get

A clear, unsentimental read on where fit is strong and where it is falling short, and a sequence of experiments designed to find out — quickly and cheaply — what will close the gap.
