Who should work with us

We work with very few companies. This is how we choose them, and how you should choose us.

Founders who hold the whole call

We work with founders and CXOs who own growth end to end: from product-market fit and growth engine design to marketing, as one system. The biggest gains rarely sit inside a single function. They sit in the decisions that cross them, and those need someone with the authority to make them.

Founders who seek truth at face value

The right partner is attached to the outcome, not to the story. Ready to hear that a favourite channel is not working, that a cherished feature is not why users stay, or that fit is weaker than the dashboard suggests, and to act on it, fast. Every answer is a reason to iterate, not a verdict to defend.

Founders ready to invest in finding out

Truth costs something: measurement, experiments, holdouts, time. Our engagements are annual, and the commitment begins at diagnosis. We work best with leaders who see that as an investment in every rupee they will spend afterwards.

Founders who read this far

Patience for depth is the best predictor of a good fit we know. If you have read through who we are, how we think and what we overlook as an industry, and found yourself nodding, or arguing, we are probably already speaking the same language.

An honest warning

In the wrong environment, we are more trouble than we are worth.

We question assumptions, resist hierarchy and pursue the truth almost perversely. If an organisation values harmony over accuracy, signalling over learning, or authority over ideas, we are a poor fit, and both sides will know it quickly. That is not modesty. It is a filter.

If you care about intellectual honesty, decisions that survive contact with reality, and systems that improve through feedback, we will probably get along.
