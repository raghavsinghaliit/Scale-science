Data & Data Science

Seeking truth with data. Most companies have more data than ever, and less certainty about what it means.

Bottlenecks and elasticities are usually hiding in data a company already has. The problem is rarely volume. It is that the data was never designed to answer the questions that matter: what did we cause, what would have happened anyway, and where will the next rupee work hardest?

Instrument for truth, not for reports

Most tracking is built to fill dashboards. We look at what you capture, at what grain, and what it silently blends together — organic with paid, brand with non-brand, first purchase with lifetime value. Blind spots in instrumentation become blind spots in every decision made on top of it.

One view of the customer

Marketing, product, CRM and offline teams often hold separate, conflicting pictures of the same customer. We help build a single view — one identity, one source of truth — that acquisition, retargeting, remarketing and personalisation all draw from. When every team acts on the same reality, spend stops working against itself.

Feed the machines better signals

Ad platforms and optimisation tools learn from what you send them. Send them installs and clicks, and they find more installs and clicks. Send them value — margin, predicted lifetime value, genuine incrementality — and they find customers who are worth acquiring. The quality of your signal sets the ceiling on what any algorithm can do for you.

From analytics to causation

Analytics describes what happened. Causal measurement explains why. We build the habit of test and control — holdouts, experiments and counterfactuals — so the business knows what each activity actually changed, not just what it touched.

Data science where it pays

Models earn their place when they move money: predicting affinity so each message reaches the user it helps, estimating incremental value per user rather than per campaign, and mapping how each lever responds as spend rises. That is how a CRM engine can maximise revenue without burning the channel.

Data is not the answer. It is how you find out whether your answer is true.

What we do

We audit how your company instruments, structures and uses data; find the gaps between what you measure and what you need to know; and work with your team to build the measurement and models that make every other growth decision sharper.
